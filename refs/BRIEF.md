# Lion Forum Institute — website brief

This file is the brief. The prospectus is a fact sheet. Oryzo is the craft bar. Do not reverse those roles.

## Goal

Build a public website for the Lion Forum Institute from a blank repo.

One sentence the site must make obvious in the first screen:

> The Lion Forum Institute exists to ensure the technologies of tomorrow strengthen American families today.

## Audience

Primary: foundations, technology companies, university and health-system partners, and senior public officials who might fund or join a pilot.

Secondary: prospective board members, Lion Forum attendees, press.

Not: retail donors, consumer product buyers, people looking for a joke.

## Tone

Confident, civic, spare. Kennedy-compound seriousness without a white paper.

Allowed: short, declarative sentences. White space. Photography. Quiet motion.

Forbidden: prospectus prose, grant language, satire, fake-AI product humor, startup hype, “powered by AI*” jokes, mascots, slogans that are not in this brief.

## Quality bar

**https://oryzo.ai/** (Lusion).

Capture it before you design. Compare our rendered pages to those captures, unlabeled.

Win on craft only:

- one idea per scroll scene
- extreme copy compression
- few typefaces, few colors
- motion and hover as hierarchy
- full-bleed visual first, labels second
- a section understandable in one glance

Do not copy from Oryzo:

- the cork-coaster joke or product-launch parody
- olive + orange system
- WebGL for its own sake
- fake model cards, BibTeX, “open-weight” gags
- any tone that would make this institute look like a studio bit

If a critic cannot tell our page from a dumped PDF, we lose. If a critic thinks we remade Oryzo with different nouns, we lose.

## Prospectus rule

`refs/prospectus.pdf` is source material only.

Extract facts. Rewrite for the web. Never transcribe section titles, paragraph density, or board-bio blocks onto the homepage.

Hard limits:

- Homepage body copy: 80–120 words total
- Any section: at most 3 short lines of supporting text
- Pilot card: Problem / Approach / Goal, one line each
- Person: name, one title line, no paragraph until a detail view

If a page needs more than a short scroll of text, split it or cut it.

## Brand (invent, then stay there)

Start from the prospectus, not from Oryzo.

- Navy + cream + one accent. No olive, no orange-from-Oryzo.
- One editorial serif + one clean sans. That is the whole type system.
- Photography: real rooms, real people, Kennedy Compound / Lion Forum stage, not stock “AI future.”
- Logo: wordmark. No lion illustration unless it is extremely restrained and secondary.

## Site map

Keep it small.

1. **Home** — mission line, four pilots as cards, Lion Forum as one scene, one CTA.
2. **Pilots** — the four 2026 pilots only. Cards first. Detail on click or a short dedicated block.
3. **Institute** — what we do and how we work, as a diagram or three beats, not the prospectus “What We Do” essay.
4. **Forum** — invitation-only convening at the Kennedy Compound. One photograph, one paragraph, sponsor relationship.
5. **People** — Christopher Kennedy McKelvy, Judd Olanoff, then prospective board as a grid.
6. **Contact / partner** — the only primary CTA.

No blog. No 12-page about. No pasted org chart of every stakeholder sentence.

## Primary CTA

One: **Partner on a pilot** (or “Work with us”).

Secondary, quieter: inquire about the Lion Forum.

Do not add Donate, Shop, Sign in, or a fake product waitlist.

## Facts you may use

### Mission

Shape emerging technologies around the needs of American families. Prove that these tools can improve health, education, and work for the median family.

### How the institute works

High-impact pilots, 3 to 12 months, measurable outcomes.

For each pilot: identify a problem, match a technology partner, recruit implementation partners, measure, then publish the lesson.

Sits between technology companies / founders / labs and health systems, universities, community orgs, and government.

### Four active pilots (2026)

1. **Special Olympics: Healthy Athletes App** — turn free screenings into follow-up care (adherence, reminders, navigation).
2. **Governor Inslee: Clean Data Center Playbook** — states negotiating data centers for clean power, jobs, affordability, community benefit.
3. **Butler University: Infant Mortality in Indianapolis** — AI on maternal health data + intervention for Medicaid mothers.
4. **Secretary Cardona: AI Career Pathways** — personalized guidance from school to work for low-income students.

### Founders

- **Christopher Kennedy McKelvy** — Founding Partner, K. Ventures; Founding Partner, The Lion Forum. Army Ranger; Oculus / Meta; MPA Harvard, BS Syracuse. Kennedy family.
- **Judd Olanoff** — Founding Partner, K. Ventures; Founding Partner, The Lion Forum. MBA Stanford GSB, MPA Harvard Kennedy School, BA Amherst.

### Lion Forum

Invitation-only convening at the Kennedy Compound, Hyannis Port. Founders, investors, elected officials. The Institute is a major sponsor and uses it to recruit partners and share results.

### Why now (one sentence max on the site)

AI is moving faster than public institutions can absorb it. If trusted organizations do not translate these tools into public benefit, the debate hardens into fear and elite-only adoption.

## How to split the gauntlet

Independent pieces, each with a builder and a separate critic:

1. Scroll narrative / homepage scenes
2. Pilot cards and pilot detail
3. People grid
4. Forum / photography treatment
5. Type, color, motion
6. Mobile

Critics open the real render and the Oryzo captures side by side, labels stripped, and pick a winner on craft. On a loss they name the single biggest gap and send it back.

## Done-when

You stop the run. The loop does not get to declare victory because it “covered the prospectus.”

A pass looks like: someone can understand the institute in 20 seconds, the page feels as considered as Oryzo, and nothing reads like page 1 of the PDF.
