# refs/ checklist

Create this folder in the new repo before you start the loop. Critics need pixels, not a URL they summarize.

```
refs/
  BRIEF.md                  (copy of the brief, or symlink)
  prospectus.pdf            (the Lion Forum Institute prospectus)
  oryzo/
    desktop-01-hero.png
    desktop-02-midscroll.png
    desktop-03-midscroll.png
    desktop-04-lower.png
    desktop-05-footer.png
    mobile-01.png
    mobile-02.png
    mobile-03.png
    scroll.mp4              (full desktop scroll if you can)
  notes.md                  (optional: viewport sizes you captured)
```

## How to capture Oryzo

1. Open https://oryzo.ai/ in a real browser.
2. Wait out the intro. Do not screenshot the loading diagram.
3. Desktop: 1440×900 or 1920×1080. Same viewport you will use for local screenshots.
4. Take stills at distinct scenes as you scroll (hero, first product beat, mid narrative, specs/table, close).
5. Repeat at a phone width (390×844).
6. If possible, record a 20–40s scroll. Name it `refs/oryzo/scroll.mp4`.

The page is a long WebGL scroll. A single above-the-fold PNG is not enough of a bar.

## Prospectus

Put the PDF at `refs/prospectus.pdf`. Do not convert it into homepage sections.

Allowed use: names, titles, four pilots, mission sentence, Forum location.

Forbidden use: pasting “Our Why,” “What We Do,” or full bios into the site.

## Local comparison rule

Every critic round should write two things into a progress page:

- our capture (same viewport as the Oryzo still)
- the matching Oryzo still

Blind: do not label which is which in the critic’s prompt. Force a pick.

## Optional but useful

- Headshots or the two founder office photo from the prospectus (page 6)
- Lion Forum on-stage photo (page 8)

If those images are not extracted yet, stills from the PDF are enough to start. Do not generate celebrity lookalikes.
